1. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinhakoFolio is designed to operate without collecting personal information from you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or contact information
  • Account credentials — there are no accounts
  • Your portfolio holdings, trade history, watchlists, notes, or photos
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), or advertising profiles
  • Location data, contacts, calendar entries, or browsing history
  • Crash reports, analytics events, or usage telemetry

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All information you enter into CoinhakoFolio — including holdings, transactions, watchlists, notes, and attached photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard sandboxed file system and the system-provided local database.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it. You retain full control:

  • Export — generate CSV or PDF reports at any time from the Settings → Manage Data menu.
  • Backup — your local data may be included in an iTunes/Finder or iCloud device backup managed by Apple under your Apple ID account settings. CoinhakoFolio itself does not initiate or read such backups.
  • Delete — uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data. You can also reset all data from Settings → Manage Data.

3. Permissions We Request Why

CoinhakoFolio asks only for the iOS permissions necessary to deliver the in-app features you choose to use. We never request permissions in the background, and each permission has a single, documented purpose:

Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Used solely when you tap "Take Photo" to attach a receipt or note image to a trade entry. The camera is not accessed at any other time. Photos you capture are saved only into CoinhakoFolio's local photo store and are never uploaded.

Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)

Used solely when you choose "Choose from Library" to attach an existing image to a trade entry. Photos are read on-demand and stored locally.

Save to Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Used solely when you tap "Save" on a photo to write it back to your library. We do not browse, modify, or delete other photos.

CoinhakoFolio does not request microphone, contacts, location, motion, tracking, or background permissions.

4. Third-Party Services

CoinhakoFolio does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social SDKs. There are no third-party trackers in the app.

Market data, prices, and quotes displayed inside CoinhakoFolio are generated for demonstration and informational purposes. They may not reflect actual market conditions, current prices, or executable quotes. CoinhakoFolio does not connect to any exchange, brokerage, or financial institution to execute trades.

5. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoFolio is intended for users 17 years of age and older (or older, where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we collect no information at all, no special handling applies.

6. International Users

CoinhakoFolio is distributed globally through the Apple App Store. Since the app does not transmit, store, or process your data on remote servers, cross-border data-transfer regulations (such as GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPL, or PDPA-SG) do not apply to your use of the app itself. Any data handling performed by Apple (such as App Store downloads or device backups) is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

7. Your Rights

Because CoinhakoFolio does not collect your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. You can exercise complete control over your data directly inside the app at any time:

  • Export your data via Settings → Manage Data → Export
  • Reset your data via Settings → Manage Data → Reset
  • Remove all data by uninstalling the app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, applicable law, or best practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted inside the app on the next launch.

9. Disclaimer

CoinhakoFolio is an information organization tool only. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or brokerage services. Investment in securities involves substantial risk, including the potential loss of principal. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Always consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
