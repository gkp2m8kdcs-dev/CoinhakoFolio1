Getting Started

  1. Open the Markets tab and tap the search icon.
  2. Search for a ticker (e.g. AAPL) and tap to open its detail page.
  3. Tap "Add to Watchlist" or "Log Trade" to start tracking.
  4. Visit the Portfolio tab to see your positions update.

Logging a Trade

  1. Go to Trade → tap the "+" button.
  2. Pick the symbol, action (Buy / Sell / Dividend), quantity, and price.
  3. Add a note or tag, optionally attach a receipt photo.
  4. Save — your portfolio updates immediately.

Exporting Your Data

Settings → Manage Data → Export. Choose CSV or PDF. Files are saved to your Files app or shared via the system share sheet. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

Resetting Data

Settings → Manage Data → Reset. Choose to wipe holdings, trades, watchlists, notes, or everything. This action is irreversible, so consider exporting first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinhakoFolio connect to my brokerage?

No. CoinhakoFolio does not connect to any exchange, brokerage, or financial institution. All trades and positions are entered manually by you. The app never executes trades on your behalf.

Where is my data stored?

All data you enter — holdings, trades, watchlists, notes, photos — is stored locally on your iPhone inside the app's private sandbox. CoinhakoFolio does not sync your data to any server. Your data may be included in your normal iCloud or iTunes device backups, which are managed by you and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Do I need to create an account?

No. CoinhakoFolio does not require an account, sign-up, or email. Install and start tracking.

Are the prices real-time?

Prices shown inside CoinhakoFolio are generated for demonstration and informational purposes. They may not reflect actual market conditions or executable quotes. Always verify current prices with your broker before making investment decisions.

Can I import transactions from my broker?

CoinhakoFolio currently supports manual entry and CSV import (Settings → Manage Data → Import). Brokerage API integration is not available and would conflict with the app's privacy-first design.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 16 or later, on iPhone. iPad is not supported.

Why does the app request Camera or Photo Library permission?

CoinhakoFolio only requests Camera and Photo Library access so you can attach receipt or note photos to a trade entry. These permissions are requested on-demand, only when you tap "Take Photo" or "Choose from Library". They are never used in the background.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email us at Mefepzavap@icloud.com with a short description, screenshots if relevant, and your device + iOS version.

Does CoinhakoFolio provide investment advice?

No. CoinhakoFolio is an information organization tool only. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or brokerage services. Investment involves risk; always consult a qualified financial advisor.
