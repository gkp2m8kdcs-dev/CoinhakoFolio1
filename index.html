Features

Everything a serious investor needs.

Market intelligence, portfolio tracking, and trade journaling — unified into one premium workflow that respects your privacy.

Interactive Charts

Candlestick, line, and mountain views with crosshair inspection. Native SwiftUI rendering — no third-party libraries.

Portfolio Tracking

Real-time valuation, allocation by symbol and sector, performance curves across multiple timeframes.

Trade Journal

Log buy, sell, and dividend transactions with notes, tags, and receipt photos from camera or library.

Dividend Tracker

Monitor dividend income and yield across positions to measure the pulse of your portfolio's cash flow.

Position Sizing

Calculate optimal share counts based on account size, risk tolerance, and stop-loss placement with real-time R:R.

ROI Calculator

Compute absolute returns and annualized yield over any time period across multiple currencies.

Sector Heatmaps

Discover opportunities with screeners, sector performance, earnings calendars, and a sentiment gauge.

Tax-Ready Reports

Realized-gain summaries by sell transactions each year, giving you a starting point for tax prep.

Multi-Currency

Convert USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, JPY, HKD, CNY, and CHF with indicative rates baked into your workflow.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device.

CoinhakoFolio is built around a simple principle: financial data is personal. We don't ask for an account, we don't sync to the cloud, and we don't track you.

  • ✓No accounts, no sign-ups, no email collection
  • ✓No cloud sync — everything stored locally on your iPhone
  • ✓No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no telemetry
  • ✓Export or reset your data anytime from Settings
  • ✓Camera and photo library used only for your receipts and notes
  • ✓Full App Store compliance — no hidden networking
